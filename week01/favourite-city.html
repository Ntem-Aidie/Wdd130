"Favourite City"

New York is a wonderful city with amazing views and buildings. Its famous subways and city park is a main attraction which fully captures the true essense of New York. It is that of high-spirits, fast paced decisions and exciting experiences. Finally, the amazing nightlife is the peak of an average New York experience, from the dazzling city lights, tall buildings, nightclubs, till even the mini concerts being hosted in dimly lit bars. New York truly has it all.
